CITATION 10

“Valentina, la bellezza del rock”

L'Arena, byline Lui.Sa. (Luigi Sabelli) · two separate, identically-headlined pieces: Sat 24 Jan 2009 and Fri 27 Feb 2009, both p.52, Giorno e Notte/Spettacoli

Resolved: this citation is actually two separate, independently dated L'Arena pieces that reuse the same headline formula and byline for the same organ trio, five weeks apart: 24 Jan 2009 (Villa Venier, Sommacampagna) and 27 Feb 2009 (Cantine dell'Arena, with Manuel Carli added). Both dates and mastheads are now confirmed by their own full-page scans. A previous caption on this page said "Villa Venier, Sommacampagna" — that text was correct for the 24 Jan piece, but at the time it was illustrated by the WRONG image: a genuine but unrelated L'Arena clipping about a different event, "Majakovskij: Serata funk/rock con il trio Black" (13 Nov 2009, guitarist Simon Beefsat, drummer Chris Tooker, no Bruno Marini or Big Caesar). That image has been moved to its own entry below instead of left misfiled here.
