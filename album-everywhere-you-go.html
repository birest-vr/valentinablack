Single · D-Vine Sounds · 2016

Everywhere You Go

Buy / Stream / Download SpotifyBeatport (Curtis Gabriel Remix)Apple MusicOther Stores
Credited to: Valentina Black, Dantiez Saunderson & Khila
Remix: Curtis Gabriel
Label: D-Vine Sounds

Reached the Beatport and Traxsource genre Top 100 charts in the weeks after release, and received airplay on BBC Radio 1Xtra, featuring in the “Music Played” tracklist of MistaJam’s show on 24 February 2016. It remains her most-streamed track on Spotify.

Released as part of the Red Room EP on D-Vine Sounds, alongside two further mixes and the title track.
